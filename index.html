Before we turn on your camera

PrepPulse can watch how your body responds while you practise, compared with your own calm baseline. Here is exactly what that means:

  • What we measure: your pulse, your breathing, and heart-rate variability (the small changes in time between heartbeats). The software reads these from tiny colour changes in your skin and small movements of your chest in the camera image.
  • Your video stays here: it is processed on this computer, never leaves it, and is never saved.
  • One check online: when a session starts, the measurement software (from a company called Presage) contacts Presage's servers to check this app's licence key and download its measurement models. No video or results are sent. Its usage reporting (telemetry) is turned off.
  • Your results stay here too: they are saved only on this computer, and you can delete them any time in Settings.
  • Not a medical tool: PrepPulse does not diagnose anything or give medical advice. It only shows how your readings change compared with your own baseline.

Before you begin

  • Sit back so your shoulders and upper chest are in view of the camera.
  • You can hide the camera preview. Measurement keeps running without it.
  • If you lean in or move out of view, measurement pauses. That is fine: just carry on with the test. Paused moments are shown honestly as "not measured" afterwards; nothing is filled in or guessed.
  • During the test you will not see any pulse or breathing numbers, only whether measurement is running.
